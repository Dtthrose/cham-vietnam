How to brew

One drip bag, one cup, about three minutes

  1. OpenTear along the dotted line at the top of the bag.
  2. HangPull out the paper hooks and hang the bag on the rim of your cup.
  3. PourWet the grounds with a little hot water (about 90°C), wait 20 seconds, then pour slowly to about 150 ml.
  4. EnjoyLet it drip, lift the bag out, and add condensed milk if you want it the Vietnamese way.
