Picture & story

“Then the laughter and the coffee come right back.”

In Hà Nội's Old Quarter, a railway runs between rows of narrow houses. Small cafés set out low stools right beside the tracks. When a train is due, everyone pulls their stools back, the carriages pass close enough to feel the air move, and then the street settles again.

Scent & taste

Tear the top of a bag and the scent of fresh coffee reaches the room before the water does. Then comes the taste: Chiềng Ban Morning Mist, 100% Arabica from the misty hills of Chiềng Ban, Sơn La, in northwest Vietnam. Roasted by Quiet Altitude and packed in single-cup drip bags.
