The story

Coffee, one step from the rails

In the old quarter of Hà Nội, a railway runs between rows of narrow houses. People live a few steps from the tracks, and small cafés set out low plastic stools right beside them.

You sit, order a cà phê sữa, and watch the street go about its day. When a train is coming, the owner calls out, everyone pulls their stools back, and the carriages pass close enough to feel the air move. Then the stools come back out and the conversation picks up where it stopped.

Brew one of the drip bags in this box, and take a minute to sit with it the way people do here.
